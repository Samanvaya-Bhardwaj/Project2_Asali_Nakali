Phase 1 · The four databases

Databases: see and change the data

The project keeps its data in four separate databases, split over two PCs. PC1 holds the pharmacy sales (DB1) and the distributor shipments (DB2). PC2 holds the national drug registry (DB3), the complaints and lab reports (DB4) and the audit ledger. On this page you can look inside them and add, change or delete records. Every change is also used by Check a medicine and by Matching & cleaning (Phase 2), and is copied to the other PC.

Tool D · One PC goes offline

What happens if PC2 is lost

PC2 connected

If the regulator's PC stops answering, the system does not fail: it answers from the data it can still reach and lowers its confidence. Press the button to cut PC2 off for a test, and press it again to reconnect.

Both PCs are reachable, so every result uses all the databases.

No confidence penalty is applied.
