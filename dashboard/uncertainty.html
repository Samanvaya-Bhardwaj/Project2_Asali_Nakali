Phase 2 · Data integration under uncertainty

Matching and cleaning messy data

The same medicine pack is recorded in four databases, and the records are often wrong: a mistyped code, a camera misreading 0 as O, the same sale saved twice, two databases giving different prices. This page works out which registered product each messy record belongs to, and cleans the databases. Codes that match no registered product are kept as evidence of fakes.

Match a code to a registered product

Use this to find out which product a messy code really means. Enter the code on the left (the other boxes are optional, but they help), then press Find matching products. The right side lists every product it could be, with the clues for and against each one, and the chance that it is not a registered product at all, which is what a fake looks like.

Find all records of a product

Use this to go the other way: start from a registered product and find every sale (DB1), shipment (DB2) and complaint or lab report (DB4) that is about it, including records where its code was saved with mistakes. Type or pick a product code and press Find its records.

Why we chose this matching method

Use this to justify the method. Each method was tested on records with known answers. The chosen method (highlighted) combines the code with clues from the other databases: was the product shipped to that pharmacy, does the price fit, do the dates fit, does the label agree.

4

Clean the databases

Use this to find and fix problems in the stored data. Every problem gets a suggested fix, a confidence and a reason. Fakes are never “fixed” into a real product: they are kept and flagged.

  1. Press Find problems to check every record in DB1–DB4.
  2. Press Fix the safe ones to apply the fixes we are sure about.
  3. In the table, decide the ones marked needs your decision.
