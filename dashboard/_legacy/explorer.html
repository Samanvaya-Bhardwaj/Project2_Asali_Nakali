How to Host Databases Across 2 Different Physical PCs

In real-world public governance, pharmaceutical entities (retailers, distributors, and the statutory drug controller) operate on physically isolated networks. Asali-Nakali natively supports running on 2 separate physical computers over your local Wi-Fi / Ethernet LAN.

PC 1: Retail & Supply Chain Node Port 8011

Hosts DB1 (Retail POS NoSQL) and DB2 (Logistics ERP SQL).

# Run on Laptop 1:
python3 scripts/run_cluster.py --mode pc1
# Or manually: python3 api/node_pc1.py --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8011

Find your LAN IP via ifconfig or ipconfig (e.g. http://192.168.1.15:8011).

PC 2: Regulatory Authority Node Port 8012

Hosts DB3 (CDSCO Master MDM), DB4 (Ministry REST), and Audit Ledger.

# Run on Laptop 2:
python3 scripts/run_cluster.py --mode pc2
# Or manually: python3 api/node_pc2.py --host 0.0.0.0 --port 8012

Enter Laptop 2's LAN IP below (e.g. http://192.168.1.20:8012) and click Connect.
